1. What PISTA is, and is not

PISTA searches for the cheapest order in which to fly a multi-city round trip. It is a search tool only. It does not sell tickets, take payments or act as a travel agent, and you never enter into a booking contract with PISTA.

2. Prices and results

Prices come from third-party flight data at the moment you search. They are indicative: they can be wrong, change before you book, or no longer be available. Always check the price and conditions on the booking site before you pay. PISTA is not responsible for any difference between the price it showed and the price you are offered.

3. Booking

Booking links open Aviasales. Any booking you make is a contract between you and Aviasales, the airline or the travel agent you buy from, under their terms. Changes, cancellations, refunds and disruption are handled by them. PISTA may earn a commission from Aviasales on bookings made through its links, at no extra cost to you.

4. Credits

Credits are a free allowance that limits how many searches each person can run, because every search costs PISTA real money. They have no monetary value and cannot be bought, sold or transferred. Unused credits do not carry over to the next month. The allowance, the cost of a search and the refund rules for failed searches (described on the Help page) may change at any time.

5. Your account

Give accurate information, keep your password to yourself, and keep to one account per person. You are responsible for what happens under your account. You can delete it at any time from My Account.
