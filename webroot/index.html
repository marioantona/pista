About PISTA

Welcome to PISTA, your go-to guide and routing engine for backpacking and multi-city European travel. We know how frustrating it is to manually piece together complex itineraries. That's why we built a proprietary algorithmic flight builder tailored for digital nomads and budget travelers.

How It Works

  • 1. Choose your parameters: Tell us your origin, flexible dates, how many places you want to visit, and how long you want to stay in each.
  • 2. The Algorithm Runs: Our custom search algorithm analyzes thousands of route combinations to find the cheapest logical path.
  • 3. Book Securely: Review our route suggestions, then click through our trusted partner links to book your tickets securely.

How credits work

Every search asks a flight data provider for live prices, which is what a credit pays for. A basic search costs 2 credits, and checking the price of a saved trip costs 1. Searches with more destinations, or with destinations you insist on visiting, have to check far more routes, so they cost more — the price is always shown above before you search. Registered accounts get 10 credits a month, which reset on the 1st.
