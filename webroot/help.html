Getting Started

What is PISTA?

expand_more

PISTA (Passenger Itinerary Self Ticketing Algorithm) is an intelligent flight booking platform that helps you search for flights, plan routes, and manage your travel itinerary efficiently. Our system uses advanced algorithms to find you the best flight options based on your preferences.

Do I need to create an account to use PISTA?

expand_more

You can search for flights without an account, but creating an account allows you to save your searches, manage bookings, and receive personalized recommendations. Sign up is free and takes less than a minute.

How do I create an account?

expand_more

Click the "Login / Sign Up" button in the top right corner of the homepage. You can register using your email address or sign in with Google or Apple. Follow the prompts to complete your registration.

Booking Flights

How do I search for flights?

expand_more

Click on "Search Flights" from the sidebar on the homepage. Enter your departure city, destination, travel dates, and number of passengers. Our system will search across multiple airlines to find you the best options.

Can I book multi-city or round-trip flights?

expand_more

Yes! PISTA supports one-way, round-trip, and multi-city itineraries. Use the "Plan Route" feature to create complex itineraries with multiple stops and connections.

How far in advance can I book a flight?

expand_more

You can typically book flights up to 11 months in advance, though this varies by airline. We recommend booking 2-3 months ahead for domestic flights and 3-6 months for international travel to get the best prices.

What payment methods do you accept?

expand_more

We accept major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), debit cards, PayPal, and Apple Pay. All transactions are encrypted and secure.

Managing Bookings

How do I view my booking confirmation?

expand_more

After booking, you'll receive a confirmation email with your itinerary and booking reference. You can also view all your bookings by logging into your account and going to "My Bookings".

Can I cancel or modify my booking?

expand_more

Cancellation and modification policies vary by airline and fare type. Log into your account, go to "My Bookings", and select the booking you wish to modify. Please note that changes may incur fees depending on the airline's policy.

What if my flight is delayed or cancelled?

expand_more

If your flight is delayed or cancelled, you'll receive notifications via email and SMS (if enabled). Contact the airline directly for rebooking options. You can also reach out to our support team for assistance.

Account & Security

How do I reset my password?

expand_more

Click on "Login / Sign Up", then select "Forgot Password?" on the login page. Enter your email address and we'll send you instructions to reset your password.

Is my payment information secure?

expand_more

Absolutely. We use industry-standard SSL encryption to protect your data. We never store your full credit card information on our servers. All payment processing is handled by certified secure payment gateways.

Can I delete my account?

expand_more

Yes, you can delete your account at any time from your account settings. Please note that you should cancel any active bookings before deleting your account. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
